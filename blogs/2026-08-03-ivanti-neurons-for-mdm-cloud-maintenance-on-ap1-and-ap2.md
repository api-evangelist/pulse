THIS IS A SCHEDULED EVENT Aug 3 , 10:30 - 12:00 UTC Jul 20 , 13:34 UTC Scheduled - Ivanti will be performing an upgrade maintenance on AP1 and AP2 Notification services for on-premise Connectors and Sentries. Impact During the maintenances, MDM clusters in AP1 and AP2 regions will be impacted in the following ways: Administrators and users attempting to log in using LDAP credentials may see intermittent failures or delays in performing authentication Administrators may be unable to register new Sentries or Connectors Configuration changes for LDAP servers and Sentries may be delayed LDAP synch

What's happened since August Patch Tuesday It's been an active month between cycles. Since Microsoft's August 11 release, five Microsoft CVEs have been confirmed exploited in the wild, and Google shipped an emergency Chrome fix for a sixth 2026 zero-day. None of this is theoretical — all six are on CISA's Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog today, and two carry active nation-state attribution.

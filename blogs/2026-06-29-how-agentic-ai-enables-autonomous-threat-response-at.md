Ivanti explores how autonomous AI systems can accelerate security threat response, noting that 40% of alerts received by security teams go completely uninvestigated while organizations handle an average of 960 security alerts daily. The piece argues agentic AI closes the gap between detection and action via autonomous vulnerability remediation, threat response, compliance monitoring, and access risk mitigation.
